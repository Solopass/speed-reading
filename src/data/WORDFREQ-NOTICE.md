# wordfreq-en-20k.txt

The 20,000 most frequent English words (lowercase letters only), most frequent
first, taken from **wordfreq 3.1.1** by Robyn Speer
(<https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq>) with `top_n_list('en', ...)`.
